Discover the top 10 iconic landmarks and spots across London

01

Tower Bridge

An iconic Victorian suspension bridge crossing the River Thames with majestic twin towers.

02

Big Ben & Parliament

The world-famous Elizabeth Tower clock and the Palace of Westminster along the Thames.

03

The London Eye

A giant observation wheel on the South Bank offering 360-degree panoramic views of London.

04

Buckingham Palace

The official London residence of the UK's sovereign, famous for the Changing of the Guard.

05

St. Paul's Cathedral

A historic Anglican cathedral with a world-renowned dome standing at London's highest point.

06

Natural History Museum

A magnificent museum in South Kensington housing millions of life and earth science specimens.

07

Royal Observatory Greenwich

Home of Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) and the famous Prime Meridian line (0° Longitude).

08

Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew

A UNESCO World Heritage site featuring vast glasshouses and the world's largest botanical collection.

09

Hyde Park

One of London's eight Royal Parks, famous for the Serpentine Lake, Rose Garden, and Speaker's Corner.

10

The Shard

A 72-storey skyscraper designed by Renzo Piano, standing as one of the tallest buildings in Western Europe.
